More integrations means more OAuth wiring. Corsair turns each service into a plugin. Install it, call `corsair.<plugin>.api.*`, expose the same endpoints to your agent.

Under the hood it's REST, not MCP-only. Agents, your backend, and a customer dashboard all hit the same layer.

## Hub

GitHub's OAuth redirect can't point at `localhost` in production. Your user isn't signed in yet when they need a connect page. Hub hosts those URLs. Add a `hub` block to `createCorsair`. Tokens still save to your database, encrypted under your KEK.

## Developer experience

The dev tunnel exposes only your Corsair handler on a public URL. OAuth and webhooks reach it the same way they will after deploy. You don't need ngrok.

Going live: swap `ck_dev_…` for `ck_prod_…`, register your delivery URL, redeploy. [Development and Production](/hub/environments) walks through it.

## For your users

Action needs Gmail, user hasn't connected Gmail. Call `createLink()`, send them to Hub's connect page, retry the action. They sign in when they need to, not all at once on a settings page.

See [Connect / OAuth](/management/connect).
